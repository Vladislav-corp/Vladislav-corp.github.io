# Tallshot: Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 August 2026**

## Short version

Tallshot has no network code. Nothing you stitch ever leaves your iPhone.

## What Tallshot does with your data

Tallshot stitches screenshots you pick into one long image. All processing happens
on your device, offline.

* **Screenshots you select.** Read only after you pick them in the system photo
  picker, and only the ones you picked. Tallshot never browses your photo library.
* **Stitched images.** Saved inside the app's own storage so you can find them
  again, and to Photos when you ask for it. You can delete every stored stitch at
  any time in Settings → Storage → Delete all stitches. Deleting removes the files
  from disk, not just from the list.
* **Your originals.** Never modified. Trimming repeated interface works on a
  working copy inside the app.
* **Settings.** Your chosen export format and quality are stored on the device in
  standard app preferences.

## What Tallshot does not do

* No data is collected, transmitted, or shared. The app contains no networking code
  and no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
* No account, no sign-in, no identifiers.
* No watermarks and no resolution limits on any tier, free or Pro.

## Purchases

Tallshot Pro is a single, one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store.
Apple processes the payment; Tallshot only asks the system whether the purchase is
active. Tallshot never sees or stores payment details.

## Children

Tallshot is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.
