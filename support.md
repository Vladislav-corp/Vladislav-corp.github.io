## Frequently asked questions

**How do I restore my purchase or subscription?**
Open the app, go to Settings (or the purchase screen) and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

**How do I request a refund?**
Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

**How is my data handled?**
Our apps work on-device and do not require an account. See the privacy policy of each app on its App Store page.
